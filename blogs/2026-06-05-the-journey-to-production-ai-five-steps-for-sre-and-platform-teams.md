Mezmo outlines a five-step framework for SRE and platform teams to move production AI agents from experimental demos to reliable autonomous operations: selecting a purpose-built harness, choosing a well-scoped initial problem, engineering context thoughtfully, implementing persistent memory, and earning autonomy gradually through co-pilot, pattern recognition, and autonomous phases.
