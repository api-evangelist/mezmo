In this Builder in the loop edition, Mezmo SRE Eric Lake shares why runbook automation is the missing piece for most on-call teams, and how AURA is filling that gap. From connecting to GitHub-hosted runbooks via MCP, to generating new ones after every investigation, to storing memory so the next incident starts with context instead of a blank slate: Eric walks through what it takes to build an agent your team can actually trust.
